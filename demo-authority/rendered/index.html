What guides our content

A strong story needs substance, not just a headline.

Relevant detailsInformation that helps readers understand the subject and its effects.
Clear sourcingIdentifiable sources, with confirmed facts kept apart from speculation.
Measured languageNo exaggerated significance or overstated certainty.
Fair contextRelevant perspectives, not conclusions from isolated details.
Reader trustOpinion and promotion identified; confirmed errors addressed.

These principles are the foundation of the publication we aim to build, and the relationship we want with our readers.
