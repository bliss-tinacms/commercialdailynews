Insurance premiums increasingly shape what first-time buyers can afford, even when the purchase price stays within reach.

## Why premiums matter

Lenders include insurance in monthly affordability calculations. A larger premium can narrow a buyer's options or increase the cash required at closing.

## What buyers can compare

Quotes, deductibles, and coverage limits vary. Comparing them before making an offer gives buyers a clearer picture of the full monthly cost.
