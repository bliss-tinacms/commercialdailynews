New pricing rules are intended to make healthcare costs easier to understand, but the effects extend beyond a posted price.

## What changes for patients

Clearer estimates can help families compare options before scheduled care and prepare for their share of the bill.

## What providers are watching

Clinics and hospitals must adapt billing systems while employers and insurers assess how the rules affect plan design and provider networks.
